Orao 2007 MX Keyboard - By Walter Miraglia and Steve Gray
=====================   Updated: 2026-05-05

This is a mechanical keyboard for the Orao 2007 computer.
It is based on the original keyboard but is slightly modified.

The changes are:

- Larger SHIFT keys.
- Larger ENTER key.
- Relocated cursor/arrow keys with Inverted-T configuration.
- Relocated and re-arranged PF keys.
- 6.25U SPACEBAR, 1.5U ENTER and SHIFT keys.


MX Switches
-----------

This design takes standard MX 3-pin or 5-pin cherry-type switches.
Five-pin switches are recommended for easier alignment.


Keycaps
-------

As this layout is unique I suggest you get blank DSA or XDA keycaps
and use sticker or waterslide sheets to label the keys. There are two
Keycap Sheets; One for Light keycaps (White, Grey,Beige etc) and one
for Black keycaps. The original keyboard was used as a guide and
additional alternate labels are included such as the PF key's actual
functions, longer names for some keys, and alternate cursor arrows.

For Light keycaps I suggest using Water-slide sheets (aka decal sheets).
For Black keycaps you can use full-page sticker sheets.


Connection
----------

For Rev 1 you will need a 16-pin ribbon cable to connect to the computer pcb.
The DIP connector matches the IC socket on the Orao PCB. Rev 2 and 2b can
also use an IDC or straight Dupont connector.

 The Dupont connector can be a 90 degree header installed on either the top
or bottom of the PCB. For Dupont cables you should use the square-pin male
headers on the Orao PCB. For the IDC connecter, I have seen pre-made
IDC to DIP ribbon cables on AliExpress.
